# Xiaohongshu / RedNote

## CLI boundary

Publish-cli has no Xiaohongshu read or write transport. This entry is a self-contained procedure for an agent using its own persistent headful browser; `publish xhs info` itself does not open or inspect Xiaohongshu and never proves readiness. The agent imports and verifies a draft in that same browser context. The caller owns the choice between a plain long article and the generated-card/image-text representation. A human scans a QR code only if login requires it, reviews any generated cards/cover and the reopened draft, and retains every final publication decision.

The supported procedure begins from a long-article source. Direct image-text, video, and podcast staging remain unspecified. Nothing here authorizes a final, private, or scheduled publication action.

## Authentication

Open `https://creator.xiaohongshu.com/publish/publish` in one persistent agent-owned browser. Positively identify the authenticated creator UI before drafting. If Xiaohongshu shows QR login or a challenge, ask the human to complete it, then continue in that exact browser context; do not switch to an unrelated browser or infer readiness from cookies or local files.

Drafts are browser-local. Preserve the same browser profile through save and verification, and do not clear its data. Authentication or draft verification is unavailable when the creator UI cannot be identified, the challenge remains incomplete, or the original persistent context cannot be continued.

## Platform specification and gotchas

- Evidence boundary: the editor, import, generated-card, topic, and draft-box facts below come from a live spike completed on 2026-08-30. Xiaohongshu owns current controls, counters, and limits; re-check the live UI and stop when it differs. An observed maximum of 100 long-article drafts is not a universal limit.
- Prepare `.md`, `.docx`, or `.txt` plus a separate title; Markdown is preferred. Import replaces the entire editor body and does not populate the separate title. If no title was supplied, copy the first Markdown H1, otherwise the first non-empty line. Never invent or silently truncate a title.
- The long-article editor showed a title counter of 64 and a body counter of 10,000, with unknown measurement units. Obey the live counters. In the measured Markdown import, H1/H2, lists, and blockquotes survived; H3 became a paragraph, and bold/italic text survived without styling. Compare the entire imported result with the source and stop on any undeclared or unacceptable loss.
- Representation decision, before any conversion: when neither generated cards nor structured topics are requested, keep the plain long-article representation. Structured topics were available only in the final image-text composer reached through `一键排版` → `下一步`; that path converts the article into generated image cards and can change its template, cover, summary, title, and caption semantics. If topics are requested but the caller has not explicitly accepted that representation, STOP before selecting `一键排版` and ask: "Structured topics require converting the long article into generated image cards and the image-text composer. Do you accept that representation change?" Do not treat silence as acceptance, and never substitute plain pasted hashtags for structured topics.
- Plain long-article route: open `写长文` → `新的创作`, import the source, fill the separate title, and compare the complete editor state with the source. The accepted long-article representation is the exact visible title plus the reviewed body text, block order, and supported structure after the disclosed import behavior; do not enter `一键排版`. Use `暂存离开` only when it is still uniquely identifiable as the non-publication long-article draft action. The prior spike did not isolate the exact `保存成功` string as a plain-route-only signal, so its presence or absence alone is not success. If the draft action is missing or ambiguous, stop without trying adjacent controls. If its activation or delivery is uncertain, do not repeat it because a draft may already exist; inspect the same-profile draft box instead.
- Generated-card/topic route, only after explicit representation acceptance: select `一键排版`, review the generated card count, card order/content, template, summary, and cover, then select `下一步` to enter the image-text composer. A measured 225-character fixture became three cards, but that count is an observation, not a rule. The platform modified and truncated the title during the observed conversion. Never silently accept, repair, or reproduce a changed title: show the exact visible result to the caller and require acceptance before making it part of the reviewed representation. The final image-text title limit and measurement remain unverified. Its caption is separate from the generated cards and showed a counter of 1,000 with unknown measurement; use only caller-supplied caption text that fits the live counter.
- In the final image-text composer, resolve each requested structured topic through live `话题` suggestions and select only an exact platform match. Verify that it became a distinct topic entity rather than literal `#text`; if the match is absent or ambiguous, stop and return the visible candidates instead of guessing. Topic discovery and selection remain caller-owned, and the maximum topic count is unknown. The spike observed four selected topic entities survive reload, draft-box reopen, and preview.
- The spike did not isolate a stable exact terminal draft-save control for the final image-text composer from final/private/scheduled publication controls. This current contract therefore authorizes no terminal save interaction in that composer: do not guess, reuse the long-editor `暂存离开` action after conversion, or treat autosave/`保存成功` alone as a card/topic draft. After the accepted cards, title, caption, and topic entities are present, STOP and request current terminal draft-save calibration; do not claim that a card/topic draft was saved. Never retry an uncertain terminal action.
- Draft verification always requires the same persistent browser and a canonical `草稿箱` reopen; a toast, autosave label, URL change, reload survival, or return to a landing page alone is insufficient. For a plain draft, reopen the corresponding long-article entry and require the exact accepted title, full body text, block order, and supported structure. For a generated-card/topic draft whose safe terminal save was separately established, reopen the corresponding draft and require the accepted card count/order/content, template/cover state, exact accepted visible title, caption, and every selected topic entity. A missing, ambiguous, or differing entry is unverified: stop and do not repeat the save.
- Browser-local means the draft is not a portable cloud guarantee: losing or clearing the agent-owned browser data can remove it. Preserve that profile for human review and report this limitation with the draft handoff.
- Never click, set, or use `发布`, any final-submit equivalent, private/仅自己可见 publication, scheduled publication, or related visibility/scheduling controls. Draft verification ends after the canonical same-profile reopen and comparison; the human owns every later review and publication decision.
