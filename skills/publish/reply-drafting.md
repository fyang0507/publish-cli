# Borrowed-Reach Reply Drafting

Load this note when turning a high-fit WATCH candidate into a reply. It is about *judgment*, not command syntax.

## The bar: additive or skip

A reply exists to **borrow the audience already on a popular post** and convert a slice of it into followers who'll later see Fred's owned content. That only works if the reply earns its place in the thread. The bar is:

- **Concrete and non-obvious** — a specific insight from Fred's hands-on experience (agent harnesses, evals, forward-deployed engineering), not a restatement or a "great post!".
- **Additive** — says something the rest of the thread can't. If Fred has nothing unique to add, **skip it** rather than post noise.
- **Timely** — the conversation is still live. A reply on a dead thread borrows no reach.

The triage `score`, `reason`, and `suggestedAngle` from `publish watch x` are inputs to this judgment, not a verdict. Only draft for the high-fit few; let the rest go.

## Voice

Write the reply through the **fred-style-guide** skill. Do not improvise Fred's voice. The skill owns tone, register, and language (Fred writes for the AI community; keep it sharp and unpadded).

## Hard stop

Draft only. Present the reply text plus the target post link to the human and hand off. **Never send** — the send action is human-gated (future scope: Discord approval, then phone escalation). Stopping at "reply drafted for human" is the boundary.
